Hand-written Poker-Hand Net

A real feedforward net (matmul + bias + ReLU + argmax) for the UCI Poker Hand task, with every weight derived by hand from poker rules - zero gradient descent, zero labels read before freezing. Pick 5 cards and classify them entirely in your browser.

Encoding: 4 suit counts + 13 rank counts (order-invariant by construction). Architecture: . See DECISIONS.md and README.md in the project repo for the full hand-derivation, the zero-label validation methodology, and honest benchmark results against trained baselines. This page's forward pass (demo/forward_pass.js) is checked against the Python/numpy implementation in the project's test suite.
