## Layer 2 outputs (relu)
- `has_quad` [shape]: 1 iff some rank has all 4 of its cards in the hand (four of a kind) - at most one rank can, with only 5 cards
- `has_trip` [shape]: 1 iff some rank appears exactly 3 times - at most one rank can, with only 5 cards
- `num_pair_ranks` [shape]: count of ranks that appear exactly twice: 0, 1 (one pair / full house), or 2 (two pair)
- `straight_window_start_1` [straight]: 1 iff the hand is exactly the straight Ace-5 (ranks 1..5 all present)
- `straight_window_start_2` [straight]: 1 iff the hand is exactly the straight 2-6 (ranks 2..6 all present)
- `straight_window_start_3` [straight]: 1 iff the hand is exactly the straight 3-7 (ranks 3..7 all present)
- `straight_window_start_4` [straight]: 1 iff the hand is exactly the straight 4-8 (ranks 4..8 all present)
- `straight_window_start_5` [straight]: 1 iff the hand is exactly the straight 5-9 (ranks 5..9 all present)
- `straight_window_start_6` [straight]: 1 iff the hand is exactly the straight 6-10 (ranks 6..10 all present)
- `straight_window_start_7` [straight]: 1 iff the hand is exactly the straight 7-Jack (ranks 7..11 all present)
- `straight_window_start_8` [straight]: 1 iff the hand is exactly the straight 8-Queen (ranks 8..12 all present)
- `straight_window_start_9` [straight]: 1 iff the hand is exactly the straight 9-King (ranks 9..13 all present)
- `broadway` [straight]: 1 iff the hand is Ten-Jack-Queen-King-Ace (the one straight where Ace plays high, not low)
- `has_flush__pt3` [passthrough]: (passthrough of has_flush to layer 3)

## Layer 3 outputs (relu)
- `straight_window_any` [straight]: 1 iff the hand is a straight from one of the 9 non-Broadway windows (mutually exclusive, so this sum is 0/1)
- `two_pair` [shape]: 1 iff the hand has exactly two different paired ranks (two pair)
- `royal_flush` [combo]: 1 iff the hand is the Broadway run and a flush (royal flush)
- `class_logit_7` [output]: raw score for class 7 (four_of_a_kind); argmax over these 10 is the prediction
- `has_flush__pt4` [passthrough]: (passthrough of has_flush to layer 4)
- `has_quad__pt4` [passthrough]: (passthrough of has_quad to layer 4)
- `has_trip__pt4` [passthrough]: (passthrough of has_trip to layer 4)
- `num_pair_ranks__pt4` [passthrough]: (passthrough of num_pair_ranks to layer 4)
- `broadway__pt4` [passthrough]: (passthrough of broadway to layer 4)

## Layer 4 outputs (relu)
- `has_straight` [straight]: 1 iff the hand has 5 consecutively ranked cards, Ace playing low or high as needed
- `has_pair_any` [shape]: 1 iff at least one rank appears exactly twice (true for one pair, two pair, and full house)
- `straight_flush_nonroyal` [combo]: 1 iff the hand is a flush using one of the 9 non-Broadway straight windows (straight flush, excludes royal)
- `class_logit_2` [output]: raw score for class 2 (two_pair); argmax over these 10 is the prediction
- `class_logit_9` [output]: raw score for class 9 (royal_flush); argmax over these 10 is the prediction
- `has_flush__pt5` [passthrough]: (passthrough of has_flush to layer 5)
- `has_quad__pt5` [passthrough]: (passthrough of has_quad to layer 5)

## Layer 5 outputs (relu)

- `distinct_ranks_regime` [combo]: 1 iff all 5 cards have different ranks (no pair/trips/quad) - the only regime where straight/flush/nothing apply
- `class_logit_8` [output]: raw score for class 8 (straight_flush); argmax over these 10 is the prediction
